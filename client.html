Aftercare Instructions

Follow these instructions to protect your treatment and keep progress on track.

Daily Cleaning & Care
Rinse your aligners with cool water every time you remove them. Brush them gently with a soft toothbrush and clear soap — no toothpaste (it scratches the plastic). Brush and floss your teeth before reinserting trays.
Eating & Drinking
Remove aligners before eating or drinking anything other than cool water. Avoid coffee, tea, and colored drinks while wearing trays, as they can stain the plastic and your teeth.
Wear Time & Tray Changes
Wear each aligner for 20–22 hours per day, changing every 10–14 days (or as directed by your orthodontist). Never skip ahead — teeth need time to move safely.
What to Do If Something Feels Wrong
Mild pressure is normal. Sharp pain, a broken tray, or a loose attachment should be reported immediately. Book a same-day emergency visit or call our clinic line at (888) 555-0123.
Post-Treatment Retainer Care
After your aligner treatment ends, wear the retainer nightly for the first 6 months, then as directed. Store it in its case when not in use, and never place it in hot water.
