Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I visit the dentist for a checkup?
We recommend visiting for a routine checkup and professional cleaning at least twice a year (every six months). However, depending on your oral health needs, our specialists might suggest more frequent visits.
Do you offer treatments for dental anxiety?
Absolutely. Patient comfort is our top priority. We offer a soothing environment, compassionate care, and various sedation dentistry options to ensure you feel completely relaxed during your procedure.
What insurance providers do you accept?
We accept most major PPO dental insurance plans. Our team will happily verify your benefits prior to your appointment and file claims on your behalf to maximize your coverage. We also offer flexible financing options.
Is teeth whitening safe for my enamel?
Yes, professional teeth whitening is completely safe. We use clinically approved whitening agents that effectively remove stains without damaging your tooth enamel. We customize the treatment to minimize any potential sensitivity.
