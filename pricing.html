Frequently Asked

Pricing Questions

Will applying for Pricing affect my credit score?
Most of our partners, including CareCredit, offer a pre-qualification process that only uses a "soft" credit pull, meaning it will not affect your credit score to see if you qualify.
Do I need a down payment?
In most cases, 0% interest plans require no down payment. Some long-term Pricing options or in-house plans may require a small deposit to begin treatment.
Can I finance cosmetic procedures?
Yes! Pricing can be used for any dental treatment, including elective cosmetic procedures like veneers, teeth whitening, and Invisalign, which are often not covered by insurance.
Does my dental insurance cover orthodontic treatment?
Many dental insurance plans cover a portion of orthodontic treatment up to a lifetime maximum. We will verify your benefits during your free consultation and help you maximize your coverage.
Are there any hidden fees in my treatment plan?
No, we believe in complete transparency. Your quoted price includes everything from start to finish: all appointments, appliances, emergency visits, and your first set of retainers.
Do you offer family discounts if multiple children need braces?
Yes! We offer a sibling discount for families treating multiple children with us. Ask our treatment coordinator for details during your consultation.
What happens if my treatment takes longer than expected?
Your quoted price is a flat fee for the entire treatment. If it takes a few months longer than initially estimated to achieve the perfect result, there are no additional charges.
