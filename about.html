For over 12 years, OrthoSmile Dental has been transforming lives through exceptional oral care, advanced technology, and a compassionate approach to dentistry.

Our Heritage

A Legacy of Excellence

Founded in 2014 by Dr. Sarah Mitchell, OrthoSmile Dental was built on a simple belief: that everyone deserves access to exceptional dental care in a warm, welcoming environment.

What started as a small practice with two chairs has grown into a state-of-the-art facility with 15+ specialists, advanced technology, and a reputation for transforming smiles across the community.
